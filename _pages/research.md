Overview
------
Mineral resource estimates are built from a limited number of drillholes, so what lies between them is always uncertain. My PhD work integrates **stochastic simulation** and **machine learning**, aiming at resource models that are accurate and that make this uncertainty explicit.

Methods
------
**Geostatistical estimation and simulation.** Kriging gives a single best estimate at each location. Stochastic simulation methods such as Sequential Gaussian Simulation (SGS) and Sequential Indicator Simulation (SIS) instead generate many equally probable versions of the deposit, so the spread between them shows how confident we can be.

**Monte Carlo methods and Markov chains.** Sampling-based approaches for exploring uncertainty and building realisations.

**Machine learning.** Data-driven models that complement geostatistics where spatial patterns are complex or relationships between variables are non-linear.
